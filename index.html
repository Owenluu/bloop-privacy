bloop. Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bloop ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Bloop mobile application (the "App"). Bloop is operated by Bloop Faith Notes LLC, based in Chicago, Illinois, USA.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Bloop, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you sign in (via Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In, or email).
  • Audio recordings: Audio you record using the App (such as sermons, meetings, conversations, reflections, or interviews).
  • Transcripts and summaries: Text generated from your recordings using the third-party services below.
  • Notes and flashcards: Any text content you create or save within the App.
  • Photos you take in Bloop: If you use Bloop's camera during a recording, the photos are stored with that recording in your private library. Each photo keeps the time it was taken and the moment in the recording it belongs to. Bloop does not record where a photo was taken, and Bloop does not look at your phone's photo library.
  • Usage data: Basic technical information such as device type, operating system version, and crash reports, to help us improve the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core functionality of the App: recording, transcribing, summarizing, and organizing your content.
  • To save your recordings, notes, and photos so you can access them across sessions and devices.
  • To show your photos next to the recording they belong to. Photos are not sent to our transcription or AI providers, not analyzed, and not used to train anything.
  • To manage your subscription.
  • To improve and maintain the App.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We never sell your data.

3. Third-Party Services

Bloop uses the following services to provide its features. When you use Bloop, your data may be processed by them, subject to their own privacy policies:

  • AssemblyAI: Your audio recordings are sent to AssemblyAI to create the transcript. See AssemblyAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude): Transcript text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate summaries, key themes, and related content. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Free Use Bible API: Scripture references found in your content are looked up on this free, public-domain scripture service; the only thing it receives is the verse reference itself. See bible.helloao.org.
  • Supabase: We use Supabase to securely store your account information, recordings, transcripts, summaries, notes, and photos. Photos are held in the same private storage as your audio, and no one else receives them. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages your subscription status. Payment itself is handled by Apple; Bloop never sees your card details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Push Notifications: Used to notify you when your recording is ready. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Sign-In and Google Sign-In: If you sign in with Apple or Google, they handle authentication. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your recordings, transcripts, summaries, notes, and photos are stored in Bloop's cloud (hosted on Supabase) using industry-standard encryption, and access is locked to your account — only you can see your library. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

"Save to Photos" copies a photo into your own photo library, and "Share" hands it to the app you choose. From then on, that copy is outside Bloop.

5. Your Rights and Deleting Your Data

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: Request a copy of the data we have about you.
  • Delete recordings and photos: Deleting a recording or a photo moves it to Recently Deleted, where you can restore it for 30 days. After 30 days it is permanently destroyed. Deleting a recording takes its photos with it.
  • Delete your account: Available in the App's Account settings, or by contacting us. Deleting your account erases all of your photos along with everything else.
  • Withdraw consent: Stop using the App and request deletion of your data.

6. Children's Privacy

Bloop is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
